## 3. Accessing Your Account

### First Time Login

1. **Navigate to the Student Portal**
   - Open your browser and go to: `http://system.sclsandbox.xyz`

2. **Enter Your Credentials**
   - **Email:** The email address you used in your application
   - **Password:** Temporary password sent to your email
   - **Important:** You will be prompted to change your password on first login

3. **Change Your Password**
   - Choose a strong password with:
     - At least 8 characters
     - Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
     - At least one number
     - At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

4. **Login Successfully**
   - After changing your password, you'll be directed to your student dashboard

### Password Reset

If you forget your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link to create a new password

## 5. Accessing Course Materials (Moodle)

SCL Institute uses Moodle as the Learning Management System (LMS) for delivering course content.

### Accessing Moodle

**Option 1: Direct Access from Dashboard**
1. Login to the student portal
2. Click on any course in "My Courses" section
3. Click "Access Moodle" button
4. You'll be automatically logged into Moodle

**Option 2: Direct Moodle Login**
1. Go to: `http://sclsandbox.xyz:8080`
2. Click "Log in" in the top-right corner
3. Enter your student credentials (same as portal login)
4. Access your enrolled courses

### Navigating Moodle

#### Dashboard (My Courses)
- Shows all your enrolled courses
- Click on any course to view its content

#### Course Page Structure
Each course typically contains:

**1. General Section**
- Course syllabus
- Instructor information
- Course announcements
- General resources

**2. Weekly/Topic Sections**
- Lecture materials
- Presentations and slides
- Reading materials
- Assignment instructions
- Quizzes and assessments

**3. Resources**
- Video lectures
- PDF documents
- Links to external resources
- Discussion forums

#### Submitting Assignments

1. Navigate to the assignment section
2. Click on the assignment name
3. Read the instructions carefully
4. Click "Add submission"
5. Upload your file or write your response
6. Click "Save changes"
7. Confirm your submission

**Important Tips:**
- ⏰ Check submission deadlines
- 📁 Save files in the correct format (usually PDF or DOCX)
- 💾 Keep a backup copy of your work
- ✅ Confirm submission was successful

#### Participating in Forums

1. Navigate to the forum in your course
2. Read existing discussions
3. Click "Add a new discussion topic" to start a thread
4. Or click "Reply" to respond to existing posts
5. Be respectful and professional in all communications

#### Taking Quizzes

1. Click on the quiz link
2. Read the instructions and time limit
3. Click "Attempt quiz now"
4. Answer all questions
5. Click "Finish attempt"
6. Review your answers
7. Click "Submit all and finish"

## 8. Document Submission

### Required Documents

After acceptance, you may need to submit:
- Student contract (signed)
- Proof of identity (passport/ID)
- Proof of address
- Academic certificates
- English proficiency proof
- Visa/immigration documents (international students)
- BRP card (UK visa holders)
- Right to study verification

### Uploading Documents

1. Go to Dashboard > "Upload Documents" or "My Documents"
2. Select document type from dropdown
3. Click "Choose File" and select your document
4. Ensure file is:
   - PDF format (preferred)
   - Clear and readable
   - Under 5MB in size
   - Named appropriately (e.g., "JohnDoe_Passport.pdf")
5. Click "Upload"
6. Wait for confirmation message
7. Document status will change to "Pending Review"

### Document Status

- **Not Submitted:** Document required but not yet uploaded
- **Pending Review:** Document uploaded, awaiting verification
- **Approved:** Document verified and accepted
- **Rejected:** Document needs resubmission (check comments)

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Access

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**
- Check you're using the correct email and password
- Ensure Caps Lock is off
- Try resetting your password
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Contact support if issue persists

**Q: Can I access Moodle from my mobile phone?**
- Yes! Moodle is mobile-friendly
- You can also download the Moodle Mobile app
- All features are available on mobile devices

**Q: How do I change my email address?**
- Contact student services at studentservices@scl.edu
- Email changes require verification
- You'll need to provide proof of identity

### Courses & Learning

**Q: I can't see my course in Moodle. Why?**
- Course enrollment may take 24-48 hours after acceptance
- Check the start date - courses may not be visible until the start date
- Contact your course administrator if enrollment is missing

**Q: When do courses start?**
- Check your acceptance letter for intake start date
- Most courses start at the beginning of September, January, or April
- You'll receive a joining email 2 weeks before start date

**Q: Can I defer my course start?**
- Contact admissions@scl.edu to discuss deferral options
- Deferral requests should be made at least 4 weeks before start date
- Terms and conditions apply

**Q: How do I withdraw from a course?**
- Contact student services immediately
- Review the withdrawal policy in your student contract
- Refund eligibility depends on timing and circumstances

### Assignments & Assessments

**Q: I missed an assignment deadline. What should I do?**
- Contact your course instructor immediately
- Explain the reason for late submission
- Provide supporting evidence if applicable
- Late submission penalties may apply as per course policy

**Q: How do I request an extension?**
- Submit extension requests before the deadline
- Contact your instructor via Moodle
- Provide valid reasons and supporting evidence
- Extensions are granted at instructor's discretion

**Q: Where can I see my grades?**
- Log into Moodle
- Go to your course page
- Click "Grades" in the navigation menu
- View all your assessment scores and feedback

### Technical Issues

**Q: My document upload failed. What should I do?**
- Check file size (must be under 5MB)
- Ensure file is in PDF format
- Check your internet connection
- Try using a different browser
- If issue persists, email the document to support@sclsandbox.xyz

**Q: Videos aren't playing in Moodle. Help!**
- Ensure your browser is updated
- Enable JavaScript in browser settings
- Try Chrome or Firefox browsers
- Check your internet speed
- Download the video if option is available
